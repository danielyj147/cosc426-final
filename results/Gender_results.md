I have saved all the results of my gender experiments in the `gender_res` folder. 
The folder has 10 files, and they can be distinguished by the number of conditions I used to analyze the predictions generated in the experiment.
The first group of file followed the naming `c2_res_gender...`. For these files, I generated results using only `years` and  `severity` conditions. 
Each of the first different files corresponds to a specific sentence template as suggested. For instance, `c2_res_gender_temp0.tsv` used sentences that 
followed the pattern of template 0.
The other group of files used all three conditions: `type`, `years`, and  `severity`. They follow the naming `res_gender_temp0.tsv` to show that those are 
results for sentences which followed template 0 and so on...
